# Use and support / 使用与支持

Caixin Companion is a free personal project for subscribers who have permission to convert content for offline reading. It is independent of Caixin and has no affiliation, endorsement or publisher authorization. It does not bypass subscriptions or grant content rights. Do not distribute exported content.

This software is provided as is, without warranty. Liability is limited to the extent permitted by applicable law; this statement does not remove rights or responsibilities that law cannot exclude. Support is voluntary, with no response-time commitment.

1. Install the unzipped release using Developer mode → Load unpacked at `chrome://extensions` or `edge://extensions`.
2. Log in on the Caixin website. Open a supported Weekly issue or article and choose 导出 EPUB.
3. Keep the task tab open. It may temporarily activate article tabs to load images. If the task is interrupted, start again; article bodies are not cached.
4. Open the saved EPUB in your preferred reader. Check images, article order and any export warnings against the source. Interactive media cannot be reproduced as a full interactive experience.

If export fails, first check that the original article is fully readable, reload the extension, and try again. The task page explains unsupported pages, missing images and save failures.

Report bugs at [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/olioliverx/caixin-companion/issues), including extension version, browser version, a public page URL if appropriate, and the error text. **Do not attach EPUBs, paid article text, account details, cookies, tokens, or unredacted screenshots.** For rights concerns, open an issue without reproducing protected content. Subscription and billing questions belong with Caixin.
